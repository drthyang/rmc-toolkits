# Lacunar spinel symmetry

## The Demo's ladder (GaTa₄Se₈, 250 K, Detected SG card)

From the tightest tolerance to the loosest: P1, Cm, Cmm2, P-42₁m, F-43m. The sampling noise floor
of the site means is 0.031 Å, and every rung below F-43m lies under it: the average structure is
consistent with F-43m (No. 216, point group -43m, 96 operations in the conventional cell).

| Group | No. | Point group | Inversion | Sohncke (chiral allowed) | Polar |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| F-43m | 216 | -43m | no | no | no |
| P-42₁m | 113 | -42m | no | no | no |
| Cmm2 | 35 | mm2 | no | no | yes |
| Cm | 8 | m | no | no | yes |
| P1 | 1 | 1 | no | yes | yes |

From F-43m to P-42₁m the three-fold axes (cubic) and the F centring are lost; -4 along one axis
and the mirrors containing it survive. Neither group has an inversion centre, and neither is
chiral.

## Published phases

- **GaTa₄Se₈:** F-43m above T* = 50 K; P-42₁m below, with c doubled (Phys. Rev. Research 4,
  033123 (2022)).
- **GaNb₄Se₈:** F-43m → P2₁3 (No. 198, point group 23) at T_Q = 50 K → P2₁2₁2₁ (No. 19, point
  group 222) at T_M = 29 K, cell doubled (Phys. Rev. B 109, 144101 (2024)). Both low-temperature
  groups are Sohncke groups: a chiral arrangement is allowed there, unlike in F-43m.

The Workbench's 5 K GaNb₄Se₈ RMC run stays F-43m with no zone-boundary order: the RMC box did not
develop the reported low-temperature structure.
